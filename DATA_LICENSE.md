The processing facts in `source_fjsp_families.json` (operation order and names, eligible machines and processing times) are transcribed from Tables 6.1–6.6 (printed pp. 109–111) and Appendix 6, Tables 1–5 (printed pp. 154–155) of:

Xie Jin (谢晋). *Job shop scheduling method based on hybrid genetic tabu search algorithm* (基于混合遗传禁忌搜索算法的作业车间调度方法). PhD thesis, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, 2022.

The author and the degree-granting institution retain their rights in the thesis. This repository does not claim authorship of the industrial processing data and does not assign an open-content license to the thesis. It distributes the following:
- a structured transcription of processing facts;
- a documented fixed-machine derivative, whose order quantities and machine allocation are this repository's choices, not the source author's;
- independently written conversion and validation code.

The thesis PDF, table images and figures are not redistributed.

Please credit the source thesis and identify this repository's version when reusing the derivative.
